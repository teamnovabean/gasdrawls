Put cover art and product photos here, but only images you have the rights
to use (your own, commissioned or licensed). Then point to them in
`assets/js/gd-data.js`, for example `art: "/assets/art/issue-1.jpg"` on an
issue or `img: "/assets/art/tee.jpg"` on a product. Covers are drawn at
2:3 and products at 1:1.
